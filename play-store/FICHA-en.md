**Nombre (27/30):** My Rentals: rent & expenses

**Descripción breve (78/80):** Track your rentals: contracts, monthly rent, invoices, expenses and reminders.

**Descripción completa (2297/4000):**

My Rentals keeps everything you let up to date: flats, rooms, parking spaces, storage rooms or business premises in Spain.

🏠 PROPERTIES AND TENANTS
• A page for each property, with purchase, value and mortgage
• Tenants with their phone number to remind them via WhatsApp

✍️ CONTRACTS
• Ready-to-sign lease (home, room, parking, storage or premises) in PDF or Word, filled in with your details
• Move-in and move-out reports with photos per room, meter readings, keys and finger signatures
• Dates, rent, payment day and deposit; rent changes from the month you choose
• Annual rent update (IRAV or CPI) with the calculation and the message for the tenant
• Reminder before the contract ends; renew or end it

💶 RENT
• A receipt every month, created automatically
• Mark it "Received" with one tap, even from the notification
• Compact numbered PDF receipt in the tenant's language, ready for WhatsApp
• Reminders to the tenant after 3 and 10 days late
• Import your bank statement (Norma 43, Excel or CSV) and the app matches each transfer with its receipt

🏪 PREMISES, OFFICES AND WAREHOUSES
• Numbered monthly invoice with IGIC (Canary Islands) or VAT and 19 % withholding
• Quarterly summary for tax forms 420 or 303, in Excel or PDF

🧾 SPANISH INCOME TAX
• Report per property: income, deductible expenses, interest, depreciation, reduction and deemed income
• Properties with several owners: report per owner

📉 EXPENSES
• Property tax, community fees, insurance, utilities… and who pays each one
• Rooms: utilities are split among the tenants
• Direct debits are confirmed when you check the charge

📑 REPORTS
• Everything in Excel or PDF, with filters, or all in one Excel file
• Statement for each tenant, and all documents and photos in a ZIP

🎨 YOUR WAY
• Light or dark theme, six colours, four text sizes and high contrast
• In English, Spanish, German, French and Italian

🔒 YOUR DATA, ON YOUR PHONE
• No accounts, no ads, nothing sent to our servers
• Fingerprint or PIN lock
• Automatic backups on the phone and, if you want, in your own Google Drive

Free with one property. A one-off purchase unlocks unlimited properties, with no subscription.

The app prepares messages and opens WhatsApp: you send them. Figures and tax reports are a guide and do not replace your accountant.
